TOP KITCHEN REMODELING DESIGN TRENDS IN DFW FOR 2026

By Alonso Innovation  ·  DFW, TX  ·  2026

← Back to Blog

The kitchen is the heart of the home — and in 2026, DFW homeowners are investing more than ever in making it a space that's both beautiful and highly functional. Whether you're planning a complete gut remodel or targeted upgrades, here's what's trending.

Here are the top kitchen remodeling design trends we're seeing across the DFW area this year.

THE TOP 7 KITCHEN TRENDS FOR 2026

01

TWO-TONE CABINETRY

The all-white kitchen had its run, but 2026 is all about contrast. DFW homeowners are pairing white or light upper cabinets with deep navy, forest green, or charcoal lowers. The result feels modern, intentional, and designer-quality.

02

QUARTZ AND QUARTZITE COUNTERTOPS

Quartz remains the dominant countertop material for its durability and low maintenance, but natural quartzite is gaining serious ground in premium remodels. Homeowners love the organic veining and authentic stone feel.

03

STATEMENT RANGE HOODS

The range hood has become an architectural focal point. We're seeing custom plaster hoods, shiplap hoods, and even arched brick-surround designs in DFW kitchens. If you have a range, make it count.

04

SMART LAYOUT UPGRADES

More homeowners are opening walls and reconfiguring their kitchen layout rather than just refreshing surfaces. Removing a wall to create an open-concept flow to the living or dining room adds both space perception and value.

05

LAYERED LIGHTING

Recessed lighting alone doesn't cut it anymore. The trend is layered lighting — combining recessed cans, under-cabinet LED strips, pendant lights over the island, and statement chandeliers for ambiance and task work.

06

INTEGRATED APPLIANCES

Panel-ready refrigerators, dishwashers, and even microwaves that blend seamlessly with cabinetry are a luxury trend that's filtering into mid-range remodels. The goal is a clean, built-in aesthetic.

07

MIXED METALS AND HARDWARE

The all-chrome or all-brushed-nickel kitchen is giving way to intentional metal mixing. Warm brass or unlacquered brass hardware on cabinets paired with matte black faucets and stainless appliances creates visual interest.

💡 Alonso Tip:

Before chasing trends, think about how you actually use your kitchen. The best remodel blends what's current with what works for your daily life. We'll help you find that balance in your custom design.

WHAT TO PRIORITIZE IN YOUR KITCHEN REMODEL

Not every budget allows for every trend. If you're working with a defined budget, here's how we recommend prioritizing:

  • Layout first — a great layout beats beautiful finishes in a bad space every time
  • Cabinetry second — it's the largest visual element and sets the tone for everything else
  • Countertops third — this is where to splurge if budget allows
  • Lighting fourth — often underbudgeted but massively impactful
  • Hardware and fixtures last — the jewelry of the kitchen, easy to upgrade later
