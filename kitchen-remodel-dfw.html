WHAT YOUR KITCHEN REMODEL CAN INCLUDE

Every kitchen remodel in DFW is scoped to your space, your cooking style, and your budget. Here's what we can build.

Custom Cabinet Installation

Semi-custom and fully custom cabinet lines installed with precision. We help you choose door style, finish, and hardware that fit both the kitchen design and the budget.

Countertop Installation

Quartz, granite, marble, and butcher block countertops templated, cut, and installed to the nearest millimeter. Undermount sinks and edge profiles included.

Kitchen Backsplash

Subway tile, large-format porcelain, hand-painted ceramic, and full stone slabs. Our tile work is straight, level, and grouted cleanly — every time.

Layout & Island Changes

Removing walls, relocating plumbing and appliances, adding a kitchen island, or opening the kitchen to the living area. We handle permits and structural coordination.

Appliance Integration

Panel-ready refrigerators, built-in ovens, under-counter microwaves, and range hood installation. We rough in the electrical and gas connections to code.

Full Kitchen Renovations

Complete gut-to-stud kitchen rebuilds — new layout, new plumbing, new electrical, new cabinets, new countertops, tile, and lighting. One crew, one invoice.
