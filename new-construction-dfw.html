WHAT YOUR NEW CONSTRUCTION BUILD CAN INCLUDE

Every custom home build in DFW is managed from groundbreaking to certificate of occupancy — one crew, one timeline, one point of contact.

Site Preparation & Foundation

Land clearing, grading, utility connections, and foundation work. We ensure the site is properly prepared before a single board is set.

Framing & Structural Work

Complete structural framing to engineered plans. We frame the walls, floors, and roof with precision and to local building codes.

Roofing & Exterior Finish

Roofing, windows, exterior doors, siding, brick, stone, and stucco. The envelope is sealed tight before interior work begins.

Interior Systems

Plumbing rough-in, electrical, HVAC installation, and insulation. We coordinate licensed trade work and manage all required inspections at each phase.

Interior Finish & Trim

Drywall, painting, flooring, cabinetry, countertops, fixtures, and trim work. Every interior surface finished to the level you selected.

Final Inspection & CO

We manage the final inspection process and deliver a certificate of occupancy. The home is clean, complete, and ready the day you take possession.

New custom home construction in DFW typically ranges from $150 to $250 per square foot for mid-range finishes, and $250 to $400+ per square foot for high-end custom builds. Total cost depends on lot conditions, square footage, layout complexity, and finish level. We provide detailed written estimates after an initial consultation and site review.

Most custom homes in DFW take 9 to 18 months from permit approval to certificate of occupancy. The timeline depends on the size of the home, permit processing in your municipality, material lead times, and weather. We set a realistic schedule upfront and communicate changes as they arise.
