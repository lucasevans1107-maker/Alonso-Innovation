WHAT YOUR BATHROOM REMODEL CAN INCLUDE

Every bathroom remodel in DFW is different. We scope your project around your space, your preferences, and your budget — then build it right.

Custom Tile Work

Floor tile, wall tile, shower surrounds, and decorative accents. We cut, set, and grout every surface with precision — no shortcuts, no lippage.

Walk-in Shower Conversions

Replace outdated tub-shower combos with open, frameless walk-in showers. We handle the waterproofing, tile, glass, and fixtures from start to finish.

Vanity & Cabinet Installation

New vanities, floating cabinets, medicine cabinets, and custom millwork. Countertops in quartz, marble, or granite to match your design direction.

Plumbing Fixture Upgrades

Faucets, showerheads, toilets, bidets, and full plumbing reroutes. We work to DFW building code and pull permits when the scope requires it.

Lighting & Ventilation

Recessed lighting, vanity bars, dimmers, and exhaust fan upgrades. A well-lit, properly ventilated bathroom prevents long-term moisture damage.

Full Bathroom Renovations

Complete gut-to-stud rebuilds with new layouts, updated plumbing, fresh framing, waterproof membrane, tile, fixtures, and premium finishes.
