## Anti-Patterns Verdict

AI slop: partially. Dark video hero and committed dark palette separate this from the HomeAdvisor template. Real testimonials feel human. But three tells: (1) every section has the identical tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow label; (2) three separate identical icon+heading+body card grids; (3) every element uses the same fade-in class with identical entrance.

Deterministic scan found 2 layout-transition warnings: line 112 (nav underline width), line 943 (FAQ max-height/padding accordion). No gradient text or other hard-ban patterns detected.

## Overall Impression

Strong bones. Real video, real photos, real testimonials. The fix list removes AI fingerprints and fixes one show-stopping functional bug.

## What's Working

1. The hero: video + Bebas Neue at a restrained clamp size, dark overlay. Shows the product immediately.
2. The testimonials: named, specific, project-labeled, and displayed in a scrolling track.
3. Services grid: specific copy grounded in Fort Worth context.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Contact form submits nowhere.** JS intercepts submit, shows "Message Sent!" and resets. No fetch, webhook, or form service. Every lead is lost.

**[P1] Section eyebrow on every section.** Seven sections all follow identical tiny uppercase tracked label cadence. Most recognized AI scaffold pattern.

**[P1] Three identical card grids.** Services (9 cards), Why Choose Us (3 cards), Blog (3 cards) — all icon+heading+body structure. Why Choose Us section especially needs structural rethinking.

**[P1] No prefers-reduced-motion.** Portfolio auto-scroll, testimonial auto-scroll, scroll line keyframe, and all fade-in transitions run unconditionally.

**[P1] Reveal animation safety.** .hero-content has fade-in class which sets opacity:0. Hero headline and CTA invisible without JS.

## Persona Red Flags

Jordan (First-Timer): Clicks "View All" in portfolio, lands on Contact — wasn't ready to contact, leaves. Submits form, sees "Message Sent!", no one calls.

Casey (Mobile): No autocomplete attributes on form fields; must type everything manually. "Book Now" creates commitment anxiety.

Carmen (Skeptical Fort Worth Homeowner): about-badge is empty — no projects count or years in business visible. Portfolio images have no captions — can't tell scope, neighborhood, or budget range. "SUPERIOR CRAFTSMANSHIP" headline lands as unverifiable superlative.
