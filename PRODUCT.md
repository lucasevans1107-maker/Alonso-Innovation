# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Fort Worth homeowners at two points in the decision journey: cold discovery (comparing contractors, reading reviews, deciding who to call) and warm validation (project already in mind, looking for portfolio proof and pricing ballpark). Both arrive with a skeptic's eye — contractor trust is low by default. The job is to convert that skepticism into a phone call.

## Product Purpose

Alonso Innovation is a Fort Worth home remodeling contractor specializing in kitchens, bathrooms, home additions, painting, and flooring. The site exists to generate qualified leads: phone calls and quote requests from homeowners who are confident they're calling the right company. Success is a homeowner saying "this feels like the real deal" before they ever dial.

## Brand Personality

Bold, Trustworthy, Precise. The brand projects the confidence of a contractor who has seen everything and delivers consistently — not flashy, not aggressive, just quietly excellent. The tone is direct and specific, never salesy.

## Anti-references

- **HomeAdvisor / Angi**: lead-marketplace aesthetic — stock photo grids, generic orange/blue, bullet-list features, "Get 3 Free Quotes" energy. This site is a craftsman's front door, not a comparison engine.
- **Cheap local contractor**: clip art, crowded layouts, Comic Sans energy, starburst callouts, aggressive phone number repetition. Every pixel should signal that Alonso does this at a high level.
- **Corporate SaaS / tech startup**: too digital, too frictionless — no sense of physical materials, weight, or real craft. The site should feel like you could almost smell fresh tile grout.
- **Luxury real estate**: too untouchable and aspirational. Projects should feel achievable and well within reach for a Fort Worth family with a real budget.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work, not the pitch.** Portfolio images and before/afters carry more trust than any headline. Give them prominence; don't bury them under copy.
2. **Precision signals craft.** A contractor who is sloppy in their website is sloppy in your kitchen. Tight typography, careful spacing, and consistent detail are the credibility signal.
3. **Fort Worth, not generic.** Specific references to the city, neighborhoods, and regional context beat contractor boilerplate. Local specificity is a differentiator.
4. **Confidence without intimidation.** Bold enough to stand out from the cheap-end competition; approachable enough that a homeowner with a $30K bathroom budget doesn't feel priced out before calling.
5. **Every page earns its own call to action.** SEO articles, portfolio pages, and the homepage each land different traffic — each needs a clear, contextually-appropriate next step toward a quote.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. No stated specific user needs. Ensure sufficient contrast on all dark-mode sections (dark backgrounds with muted white text are a recurring risk). Reduced motion media query required for all scroll-triggered animations.
